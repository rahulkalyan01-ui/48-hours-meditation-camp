WHAT TO BRING

  • Comfortable, modest, loose-fitting clothes
  • One single bedsheet
  • Shawl / light blanket / warm clothing as required
  • Personal toiletries
  • Refillable water bottle
  • Notebook and pen
  • Comfortable footwear

WHAT NOT TO BRING
